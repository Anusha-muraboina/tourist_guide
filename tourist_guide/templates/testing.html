A bond of craft. A promise

Samanvaya — meaning harmony — is a design-and-build studio uniting architecture, construction and interiors under one roof. From the first pencil detail to the final brass fitting, our team carries your home, so nothing is lost between drawing board and door.

Relationships that endure, profiles that hold, builds that last tomorrow — with our clients, our craftsmen and the tradition we build upon.

WHAT WE DO

One studio, from sketch to keys

Architecture

Bespoke residences and considered design that honour vernacular Indian form — courtyards, jaalis, verandas — detailed with contemporary planning and light.

  • ◆ Concept & master planning
  • ◆ Working drawings
  • ◆ 3D visualisation & walkthroughs
  • ◆ Statutory approvals & liaison

Construction

In-house execution with our own site teams and master craftsmen. Honest materials, rigorous billing and supervision at every stage, from foundation to finish.

  • ◆ Turnkey civil execution
  • ◆ Skilled in-house masonry
  • ◆ Quality-audited materials
  • ◆ On-time, on-budget delivery

Interiors

Interiors that carry the architecture inward — carved timber, brass, terrazzo and handloom textiles composed into warm, liveable, enduring spaces.

  • ◆ Complete interior design
  • ◆ Custom furniture & joinery
  • ◆ Art & artisan curation
  • ◆ Lighting & styling
